**Known limits**
- **Going back to a version before BUH-02.19 with DeepSeek chosen for AI writing:** that version treats DeepSeek as Claude: "Write from photo" uses your Anthropic key if one is saved, or says there is no key, and Settings → AI writing will not save until you choose Claude or Gemini. Choose Claude or Gemini before going back, if you can. Gift lines are kept on products but not shown.
- **Going back to a version before BUH-02.17 while UAE VAT is on:** that version places orders without VAT (no `order_vat`, no tax invoice) and leaves the VAT documents alone. Money → VAT flags every order placed after VAT was switched on that has no VAT breakdown, and the VAT return CSV lists them; check those with your accountant. Turn VAT off before going back, if you can.
